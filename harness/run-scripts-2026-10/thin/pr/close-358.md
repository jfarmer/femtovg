Fills thinner than a pixel are drawn through exact coverage masks rasterized on the CPU into one atlas, on OpenGL and WGPU alike, instead of rasterizing every antialiased fill on the GPU here, which cost the median frame 2.8 -> 5.1 ms. #408 keeps the median frame within 0.1 % and brings the corpus frames at 1x and up 28 % nearer the exact area.
